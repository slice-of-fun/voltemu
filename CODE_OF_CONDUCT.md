# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

Volt Emulator is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and productive environment for everyone — regardless of age, background, experience level, gender, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity.

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for all.

---

## Our Standards

### Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in all communications
- Give and receive feedback graciously
- Focus disagreements on code, design, and ideas — never on people
- Welcome newcomers and help them succeed
- Acknowledge and credit others' contributions
- Accept responsibility when you make mistakes

### Unacceptable Behavior

- Personal attacks, insults, or derogatory comments
- Harassment in any form — public or private
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Trolling, inflammatory comments, or deliberate disruption
- Sexual or discriminatory language and imagery
- Sustained aggressive or intimidating behavior
- Any conduct that would be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces:

- GitHub repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, code reviews)
- Official Discord / community chat
- Community forums
- Events or meetups representing Volt Emulator

---

## Enforcement

### Reporting

Report violations by emailing: **conduct@volt-emu.dev**

All reports are handled confidentially. Include:
- Your contact information
- Description of the incident
- Any supporting evidence (screenshots, links)

### Response Process

| Severity | Response |
|----------|----------|
| Minor (first offense) | Private warning |
| Moderate | Temporary ban from community spaces |
| Severe | Permanent ban |
| Illegal activity | Reported to appropriate authorities |

Maintainers will respond to reports within 72 hours.
